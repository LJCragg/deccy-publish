# De-novo vs Emu based 16S discussion summary

## Pre-threepoint comparison
- De-novo resolved 857 pooled OTUs versus 353 for Emu based.
- De-novo captured 288 pooled genera versus 242 for Emu based.
- Mean observed OTU richness per barcode was 690.7 +/- 49.2 for De-novo and 230.2 +/- 36.2 for Emu based.
- Mean Shannon diversity was 4.62 +/- 0.27 for De-novo and 4.13 +/- 0.38 for Emu based.
- Global OTU cluster sizes were smaller in De-novo (mean 283, median 48, mode 19) than in Emu based (mean 793, median 136, mode 28).
- Genus overlap before filtering was 193 shared, 95 De-novo only, and 49 Emu based only.

## Post-threepoint comparison
- De-novo retained 357 pooled OTUs and Emu based retained 245 after threepoint filtering.
- Post-threepoint pooled genera were 153 for De-novo and 177 for Emu based.
- Mean observed OTUs per barcode fell to 313.3 for De-novo and 199.3 for Emu based.
- Mean Shannon diversity post-threepoint was 4.31 for De-novo and 4.07 for Emu based; Simpson was 0.968 and 0.962, respectively.
- Post-threepoint mean and median OTU cluster sizes remained smaller in De-novo (mean 638, median 162) than in Emu based (mean 1129, median 276), while the modal cluster size was 63 in De-novo versus 53 in Emu based.
- Genus overlap after filtering was 125 shared, 28 De-novo only, and 52 Emu based only.

## Threepoint retention
- De-novo retained 41.7% of pooled OTUs, 53.1% of pooled genera, and 93.9% of pooled reads.
- Emu based retained 69.4% of pooled OTUs, 73.1% of pooled genera, and 98.8% of pooled reads.

## Discussion note
The pre-threepoint comparison shows the broader viewpoint generated by reads-first clustering. The post-threepoint comparison then tests how much of that viewpoint survives a strict reproducibility filter.
After threepoint filtering, De-novo still retains more OTUs, but Emu based retains more pooled genera. That is the clearest signal that the two workflows are not just differing in count, but in the level at which they compress the community.
