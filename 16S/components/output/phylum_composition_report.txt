================================================================================
INTERPRETATION GUIDELINES
================================================================================

Bacillota:
Enriched in fuzz cohort - consistent with stress-tolerant, anaerobic,
and spore-forming lineages under reduced/disturbed soil conditions.
Higher variance in fuzz indicates heterogeneous microbial response.
Citations: Rivalland et al., 2022; Palmer, 2019; Yutin & Galperin, 2013

Pseudomonadota:
Stable across cohorts but more variable in fuzz - reflects metabolically
flexible heterotrophs persisting across disturbance gradients.
Increased variance suggests patchy micro-environmental conditions.
Citations: Fierer et al., 2007; Delgado-Baquerizo et al., 2018

Bacteroidota:
Depleted in fuzz cohort - indicates functional simplification and
environmental filtering. Associated with complex carbon degradation,
rhizosphere activity, and soil functional capacity.
Citations: Liu et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2019
